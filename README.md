## The three sentences that matter most

1. **AI audit is not a new discipline — it is your existing discipline applied to a new class of asset.** The chain is unchanged: Requirement → Risk → Control → Evidence → Test → Finding → Recommendation.
2. **You audit whether appropriate validation happened; you do not personally perform statistical model validation.** Confusing these two is the single most common failure mode for new AI auditors.
3. **In Malaysia today, most AI-specific expectations are guidance or voluntary — but the *existing* mandatory requirements (RMiT, FSA s.134 secrecy, outsourcing, PDPA) already bite on AI systems.** Audit against what is enforceable first.

## Notation used throughout

- 🔴 **MUST-KNOW** — core concept; you will be asked about this
- 🟡 **NICE-TO-KNOW** — useful context; not required to run an audit
- ⚖️ **MANDATORY** — enforceable regulatory requirement
- 📘 **GUIDANCE / DISCUSSION** — regulator-issued but not binding
- 🏛️ **INDUSTRY FRAMEWORK** — issued by an industry body
- 📗 **VOLUNTARY STANDARD** — international standard, adopt by choice
